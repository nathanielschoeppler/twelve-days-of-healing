Before you begin

Take a slow breath and read these. There's nothing to be nervous about.

Philippians 4:6–7
“Do not be anxious about anything, but in everything by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known to God. And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus.”

Turn anxiety into prayer with thanksgiving, and receive God's peace that passes understanding.

1 Peter 5:6–7
“Humble yourselves, therefore, under the mighty hand of God so that at the proper time he may exalt you, casting all your anxieties on him, because he cares for you.”

You don't have to carry any of it alone. Hand it over; He cares for you.

Matthew 6:25, 34
“Therefore I tell you, do not be anxious about your life, what you will eat or what you will drink, nor about your body, what you will put on. Is not life more than food, and the body more than clothing?… Therefore do not be anxious about tomorrow, for tomorrow will be anxious for itself. Sufficient for the day is its own trouble.”

Don't worry about daily needs or about tomorrow's troubles.

Isaiah 41:10
“Fear not, for I am with you; be not dismayed, for I am your God; I will strengthen you, I will help you, I will uphold you with my righteous right hand.”

God is with you: your strength, your help, and your support.

Here's the plan

  • First, a short singing lesson (about 10 minutes) with your vocal coach: breathing, a warm-up, and practice on the tricky parts.
  • Then the real recording. Sing along with the words on screen.
  • The app does the rest: it lines your voice up with the music, tunes every note, and mixes the finished song.
Where does your voice sit?
Not sure? Start with Middle. Your coach will help you check in the warm-up.
Getting the music ready…

Time for the real deal

  • Headphones in. The music plays in your ears so the microphone only hears you. AirPods are fine; the app corrects their small delay.
  • A quiet, soft room, like a bedroom or a closet full of clothes.
  • Stand up, with water nearby. The whole song takes about 5 minutes; the quick version about 3.

Here's our studio plan, Hannah. We'll record three short parts, and I'll build the whole song from them.

  1. Part 1, the opening lines. Only “On the first day of healing, my chiropractor gave me”, then the second day, the third, all the way to the twelfth. You get a short rest after each one.
  2. Part 2, the early countdown. “Four x-ray views, Three punch cards, Two posture tips, And one perfectly aligned spine”. This is the gentler tune from the first verses.
  3. Part 3, the big countdown. From “Twelve months of wellness” all the way down to “And one perfectly aligned spine!” Give “Fiiive” everything you've got.

My pro tip: since each line gets reused in several verses, sing every line with the same bright, steady energy, as if it's the best verse of the song. Keep going straight through; the music and the words on screen tell you when each part starts.
